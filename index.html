Chi sono

I’m Ricardo, and I say this about myself with a straight face: I am addicted to lasagna. It started as a love of Italian culture and went straight to the part you can eat — the Sunday table, the arguing about ragù, the pan that comes out of the oven still bubbling.

So the word bank here is a real kitchen, not filler. Every word is something you’d actually hear over a plate: besciamella, soffritto, nonna, buon appetito. Flip on Italian brainrot mode and the meme creatures crash the dinner party too, because tralalero tralala deserves to appear in somebody’s design mockup at least once.

My favourite Italian word is prego — the swiss army knife of the language. Please, you’re welcome, after you, go ahead, help yourself, don’t mention it. One word, every occasion. It’s in the word bank, obviously.

Placeholder text has to go somewhere. It may as well be delicious.
